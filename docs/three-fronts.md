# Three Fronts

Three Fronts is a separate local 5v5 lane siege. A player controls one Vanguard; the other four allies and all five rivals use AI. It has no network matchmaking and does not read or alter Arena Elo, tournaments, quests, or cloud saves.

- Choose one of seven saber forms, then pick North, Center, or South. The form changes reach, guard or mobility instead of only changing the label. Push alongside your minions, knock down one enemy tower, then turn toward the newly exposed Core.
- WASD / arrows to move, J to slash, K to lunge, Space to guard. Aim with cursor or tap. On-screen movement and combat buttons support multiple touches.
- Return to your Core to buy kit upgrades with battle credits (button or U). Watch your stamina and reserve a lunge for an opening.
- The Core ends a fight. An unbroken stalemate is judged after ten minutes. No progression persists between siege matches.
- Use Follow / Full map to choose a close camera or full field. Narrow screens start in Follow with a minimap; reduced motion turns off decorative contact rings and walking flourish.
