# Arena AI Force loadouts

Open **Teams & ranks** and expand **AI Force loadout** under Blue or Pink. Pick up to three different powers, or use **Mixed trio** for Push, Anchor, and Siphon. In Play, only AI fighters get the pool. In Spectator, both sides can use their configured pools. A side with no custom picks keeps its original primary Force. Saved Heroes retain their own kits.

| Power | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Push | Creates space with strong forward knockback. |
| Burst | More expensive close-range area impact. |
| Pulse | High-range, narrow, quick-pressure option. |
| Pull | Draws an exposed enemy closer. |
| Anchor | Briefly slows an exposed moving enemy. |
| Lattice | Buys time with a short one-hit ward. |
| Echo Step | A clear-ground reposition without invulnerability. |
| Siphon | Recovers a little stamina from an exposed enemy. |

Each AI chooses a cast for the current range, stamina and opening. A cast can still be interrupted or miss. Custom pools are for unrated Arena only; Saber only disables them without erasing the selection. Stats lists released powers by name. The standalone MOBA, tournaments, quests, and historical replays do not acquire these loadouts.
